1. Formalized Functionalities by Entity:

Entity: Article

    Read (R): The Administrator lists the articles.

    Create (C): The Administrator adds an article.

    Update (U): The Administrator modifies an article.

    Delete (D): The Administrator deletes an article.

Entity: Category

    Read (R): The Administrator lists the categories.

    Create (C): The Administrator adds a category.

    Update (U): The Administrator modifies a category.

    Delete (D): The Administrator deletes a category.




2. Example Functional Path:

Feature: The Administrator adds a category

    Actor: Administrator

    Starting Screen: Category Management Page (/admin/categories)

    Action: Clicks the "Nouvelle Catégorie" (Add a category) button

    Result: Displays the category creation form
